<product_requirements>
The objective is to build and maintain a sophisticated automated trading bot for SPY options on the Webull platform, controlled via a React-based web dashboard.

**Core Functionality:**
-   **Automated Trading:** The bot should execute call/put trades based on predefined strategies: Opening Range Breakout (ORB) and Supply/Demand (S/D) zones. Purely technical trades (RSI, MACD) have been explicitly disabled.
-   **Dashboard UI:** A web interface providing:
    -   Login and session management.
    -   Widgets for Account Overview, Bot Status, Live Positions, Order History, Trading Signals (ORB, S/D, Technicals), and Manual Trade Panel.
    -   A settings page to toggle auto-trading and override profit/loss targets.
-   **Dynamic Trading Logic:**
    -   **DTE Strategy:** ORB trades use 0DTE contracts. S/D trades use 0DTE before 1 PM EST and 1DTE after.
    -   **Trailing Stop Strategy:** A two-phase system for profit-taking and risk management, with different rules for 0DTE and 1DTE contracts.
        -   *0DTE:* Phase 1 sells all but one contract at +25% profit / -25% loss. Phase 2 targets +75% profit with a +5% stop-loss on the final contract.
        -   *1DTE:* Phase 1 sells all but two contracts at +20% profit / -25% loss. Phase 2 targets +95% profit with a +5% stop-loss on the final contracts.
    -   All bot-placed orders must be Market (MKT) orders to ensure execution in fast-moving markets.
-   **Notifications:** A smart email notification system that sends one Bot Online email at market open, one Bot Finished email at market close, and immediate notifications for all executed trades. Spamming Bot Started emails outside market hours must be prevented.
-   **Data & History:** The order history must display the reason (signal) for each trade.
</product_requirements>
