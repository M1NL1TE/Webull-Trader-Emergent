<analysis>
The AI engineer progressed a Webull trading application from basic functionality to a complex, almost fully automated system. Initial efforts focused on fixing P/L calculation, adapting it from historical orders for paper trading, and introducing per-symbol breakdowns. A major phase involved integrating the user's trading bot logic, transitioning from a placeholder to actual trade execution, including ORB detection and technical analysis. Frontend visibility was enhanced with new widgets for bot status, signals, and supply/demand zones. Persistent challenges included Playwright login stability (initially fixed, then assumed stable), persistent Twilio SMS undelivered errors due to carrier blocking (leading to a pivot to email notifications), and critical bugs like the auto-trading toggle not triggering the bot immediately and the outright absence of sell logic for stop-loss/take-profit, which is the immediate focus.
</analysis>

<product_requirements>
The user initially requested a Webull trading application capable of automated SPY options trading (puts/calls based on technical analysis), with dynamic selling for profit/loss based on user-defined percentages. The app needs to support both paper and real trading modes via a UI toggle. A modern dashboard is required with login/authentication, account overview (balance, positions, P&L), trading signals, manual trade execution, an auto-trading toggle, live position monitoring, and trade history. Key security concerns include secure storage of Webull credentials and session tokens. Twilio SMS notifications were requested optionally. Later, the user explicitly asked for accurate P/L calculation for various time filters, per-symbol P/L breakdown, a frontend widget to display bot status, the implementation of the full automated trading logic (including ORB, technical analysis), real-time signal visibility, reasons for not taking trades, persistent supply/demand zones, and profit/loss target percentages in the positions widget. The latest request also included fixing overlapping supply and demand zones.
</product_requirements>
