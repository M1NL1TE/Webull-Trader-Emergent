<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build and maintain a sophisticated automated trading bot for SPY options on the Webull platform, controlled via a React-based web dashboard.

Key features and requirements include:
- Automated trading based on Opening Range Breakout (ORB) and Supply/Demand (S/D) strategies.
- A web UI for login, account overview, bot status, live positions, order history, and settings.
- Dynamic DTE (Days to Expiry) Strategy: 0DTE for ORB, and a time-based 0DTE/1DTE split for S/D trades.
- A two-phase trailing stop-loss system for profit-taking.
- All bot orders must be placed as Limit (LMT) orders for the live account.
- Smart email notifications for bot status, executed trades, and when price enters S/D zones.
- The Order History must show the signal/reason for each trade.
- A critical security vulnerability allowing login bypass was identified and needed fixing.
- A recurring environmental issue where Playwright browsers are deleted on container restart needs a permanent solution.
- The P/L by Symbol widget needed to be consolidated to show total P/L per ticker.
- The stop-loss logic needs to be updated to trigger only if a candle on a lower timeframe closes outside the zone.
- Capital allocation for trades should be doubled for accounts with a balance under ,000.

**what currently exists?**

The dashboard displays account info, bot status, positions, and order history. The bot also sends email notifications for trade executions and zone entries.
